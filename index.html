    About me

    The short version, for anyone who does not want to read a CV.

    I finished my Computer Science degree at the Arab Academy for Science, Technology & Maritime Transport in June 2026, with a 3.66 GPA and an A+ on my graduation project.

    I found data in the middle of that degree. The DEPI Data Analytics track is where it started — SQL, Power BI, and a lot of cleaning other people's spreadsheets. What I noticed was that the interesting problems were never in the chart. They were further back: the data was wrong, or arrived in a shape nobody could query, or could not be trusted enough to act on.

    So I moved upstream. I built a warehouse for four seasons of Formula 1 data to learn schema design properly, and I am now on the Samsung Innovation Campus big data track filling in the parts I had been working around: distributed processing, ingestion tooling, streaming and cloud.

    Alongside that I am interning at Finnovate, part of ICT Misr Group, on banking automation. I still write frontend when a project needs it — that is how I ended up leading the interface for my graduation project — but data is the direction I am going.
